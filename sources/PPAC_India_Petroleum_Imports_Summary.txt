Verified Records & Data:
1. India Crude Import Dependency:
   - Domestic Crude Production: ~28-29 MMT / annum
   - Crude Processing / Consumption: ~260-265 MMT / annum
   - Import Reliance: 85.6% to 88.2% across 2022-2026.
2. Middle East / Strait of Hormuz Share:
   - Imports from Persian Gulf nations (Iraq, Saudi Arabia, UAE, Kuwait, Oman):
     Account for ~45% to 55% of total Indian crude procurement.
   - Maritime corridor: 100% of Persian Gulf crude imports pass through the Strait of Hormuz into the Arabian Sea bound for western/southern Indian refining ports (Vadinar, Jamnagar, Mumbai, Mangalore, Kochi).
3. Impact of Blockade / Geopolitical Escalation:
   - Escalation of maritime insurance premiums (war risk), diversion around Cape of Good Hope (+14 to 20 days transit), and volume constraints directly impair feedstock flow to Indian refiners.
