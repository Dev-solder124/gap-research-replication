Topic: World Oil Transit Chokepoints - Strait of Hormuz
URL: https://www.eia.gov/international/content/analysis/special_topics/World_Oil_Transit_Chokepoints/
Verified Records & Data:
1. Physical Transit Volume:
   - Average flow: 20.5 to 21.0 million barrels per day (b/d).
   - Share of Global Seaborne Oil Trade: ~27% to 30%.
   - Share of Global Petroleum Liquids Consumption: ~21%.
2. Destination Markets:
   - Asian destinations receive ~82% to 85% of crude and condensate moving through Hormuz.
   - Primary destinations: China, India, Japan, South Korea.
3. Liquefied Natural Gas (LNG):
   - Roughly 20% of global LNG trade transits the Strait of Hormuz (largely Qatar).
4. Bypass Capabilities:
   - Very limited bypass pipelines:
     * Saudi East-West Pipeline (Petroline): capacity ~5.0 million b/d.
     * UAE Abu Dhabi Crude Oil Pipeline (Habshan-Fujairah): capacity ~1.5 million b/d.
   - Net unbypassable volume: >14 million b/d (>70% of transit flow) cannot be rerouted overland, making the waterway an absolute physical chokepoint.
